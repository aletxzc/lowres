# Lowres

Turn your phone's camera into retro cameras: early-2010s handheld consoles, 2000s compact digicams, disposable film cameras. Each camera, which Lowres calls a **Model**, gives your photos the look that kind of device had (its resolution, colors, noise and quirks), live in the viewfinder.

> 🚧 **Early development.** There's nothing to install yet.

## Planned for v1

| Model           | Inspired by                         | Look                                                  |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Handheld 3D** | early-2010s handheld game consoles  | 640×480, noisy shadows, washed-out colors             |
| **CCD Digicam** | mid-2000s compact digital cameras   | saturated colors, JPEG artifacts, orange date stamp   |
| **Disposable**  | 35 mm disposable film cameras       | grain, warm tones, vignetting, light leaks            |

- The effect is applied live in the viewfinder, on the front and back cameras.
- Photos save straight to your camera roll.
- Available in English and Spanish.
- Installable web app (PWA): works on iPhone, Android and desktop.

## Privacy

Your photos never leave your device. Lowres has no servers, no analytics and no tracking: every photo is processed on your phone.
